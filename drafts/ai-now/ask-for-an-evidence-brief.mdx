## Before you start

Continue the same conversation after verifying that AI Now can read the source from lesson 2. Keep the original document open for comparison.

## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the output shape">
    Ask for a brief that makes its reasoning reviewable:

    ```text
    Based only on the Atlas planning memories and atlas-planning-interviews.md, write an evidence brief about piloting an annual planning review. Do not search the web.

    Include:
    1. Findings that support a trial
    2. Findings that limit or challenge it
    3. Open questions, marked as internal questions or public research questions
    4. Assumptions, kept separate from evidence
    5. One small next step that would reduce uncertainty

    For each finding, give the source name and section, what it establishes, and what it does not establish. Do not force a finding into a category if there is no supporting evidence.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the evidence before the recommendation">
    Check one finding in the original source. For the practice material, these are useful boundaries:

    | Source | Supports | Does not establish |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Interview A | A need to spot dependencies earlier | That an annual meeting will solve it |
    | Interview B | A trial using existing notes | Acceptance of more reporting work |
    | Interview C | The need to measure a trial | Any measured benefit yet |

    Ask AI Now to revise claims that go beyond those boundaries. The three interviews are fictional practice evidence, not a market sample.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep open questions open">
    Pick one uncertainty for lesson 4. "How much preparation can our team support?" needs an internal answer. "What published evidence supports longer-horizon planning reviews?" can be researched publicly. A lack of evidence is a valid finding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  The brief separates a reason to test the idea from proof that it works. Each finding has a source, and the next uncertainty has a clear way to investigate it.
</Check>

## Why this matters

An evidence brief is more useful than a polished answer with hidden assumptions. It gives you a clear boundary between the knowledge you own and the information you still need.

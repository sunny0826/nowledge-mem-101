## Before you start

Have the evidence brief and the finding or open question from lesson 4 ready. Public research is optional; record only the work you actually completed.

## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a short decision record">
    In the AI Now task, ask:

    ```text
    Draft a short record of this Atlas planning task. Include its date, whether it is a recommendation or an approved decision, the evidence and source references, a key limitation, and one next action. Preserve any unresolved question. Do not save the record yet.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify it before saving">
    Keep source names and sections in the record itself. Add verified research links and their dates only if you used them. For the fictional interviews alone, an appropriate record could be:

    ```text
    Atlas planning recommendation — fictional practice scenario

    Status: recommendation; no pilot approved.
    Recommendation: test one joint review using existing quarterly notes before committing to an annual process.
    Basis: atlas-planning-interviews.md, Interview A describes a missed dependency; Interview B conditions participation on reusing notes.
    Limit: Interview C confirms no trial or measured benefit. These are fictional course notes.
    Next step: ask the review owner to agree a preparation limit and a measure of dependencies found before approving a pilot.
    ```

    Add the date you reviewed the record. Replace the example with your actual conclusion; an unresolved decision can still be worth saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it in Timeline">
    Copy the verified record to **Timeline** and press Enter. Confirm the saved entry appears. If AI Now already saved the same result during the task, open and check that memory instead of adding a duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it from a fresh conversation">
    Open a new **AI Now** conversation in the same Space and ask:

    ```text
    Find the Atlas planning recommendation in Nowledge Mem. Is a pilot approved? What evidence supports the recommendation, what is still unknown, and what happens next? Identify the saved record.
    ```

    Compare the answer with the memory. It should preserve the unapproved status and the source references.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  A fresh conversation finds the record with its status, evidence, limits, and next action. It does not turn your recommendation into an approved decision.
</Check>

<Tip>
Correct wording mistakes in the existing memory. When a real decision changes later, save a dated update that names the earlier record and explains the change, so you can still trace the history.
</Tip>

## Why this matters

The result becomes useful beyond this AI Now session. Future tasks and connected AI tools can start from the decision record, then return to the source or research only when they need more detail.

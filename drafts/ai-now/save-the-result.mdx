## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a short decision record">
    In the AI Now task, ask:

    ```text
    Turn this work into a decision record for Nowledge Mem. Include the decision or current recommendation, the strongest supporting evidence, an important limit, and one next action. Keep it under 120 words.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify it before saving">
    Check that it does not claim a final decision if the evidence only supports a recommendation. Keep links to the primary source and research in the task when you need the detail later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it in Timeline">
    Copy the verified record to Timeline and press Enter. Edit it later if the decision changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  You have a searchable memory that records the conclusion, its limits, and the next action without requiring the whole research conversation.
</Check>

## Why this matters

The result becomes useful beyond this AI Now session. Future tasks and connected AI tools can start from the decision record, then return to the source or research only when they need more detail.

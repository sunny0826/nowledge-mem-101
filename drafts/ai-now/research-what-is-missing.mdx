## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose one open question">
    Pick a question the interview notes and memories cannot answer. For example: what current evidence shows that annual planning reviews reduce unplanned work for operations teams?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Deep Research">
    In the AI Now chat, turn on the **Research** toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for sources, not a generic verdict">
    Keep the research scoped:

    ```text
    Research current public evidence about annual planning reviews and unplanned work for operations teams. Use the Atlas evidence brief as context, but do not treat it as external proof. Return the main findings, source links, reliability indicators, and limits of the evidence.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the research with your own evidence">
    Check whether the findings answer the open question. Keep disagreements visible instead of forcing a single conclusion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  AI Now combines the task context with cited public research and makes the evidence limits visible.
</Check>

<Warning>
Deep Research searches the web. Do not include confidential information in the research request, and review the cited sources before relying on them.
</Warning>

## Why this matters

Research is most useful after you know what is missing. Your own memories and source material set the question; current public sources help fill a specific gap.

The fictional example asks whether Atlas should pilot an annual planning review next quarter. The pilot is not approved at the start. Lesson 1 provides practice context and lesson 2 provides interview notes, so you can follow the whole case or substitute your own question.

## Before you start

- Complete the [Essentials course](/essentials).
- Configure a model in **Settings → Remote LLM**. Remote clients use the Mem server's model configuration.
- Have one relevant memory and one source, or use the fictional practice material in lessons 1–2.
- Keep the same AI Now conversation through the analysis; use a fresh conversation for the final retrieval check.

<Note>
The outcome is a traceable recommendation or decision, including what remains unknown. You do not need to force a final answer when the evidence is incomplete. Research time may extend beyond the lesson estimates.
</Note>

## What this course does not assume

The core path uses memories, one source, and a clear question. In lesson 4, choose an internal follow-up for team-specific facts or Deep Research for public evidence. You can finish the course with an unresolved question and a concrete next action.

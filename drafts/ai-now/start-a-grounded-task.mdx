AI Now works best when the task begins with knowledge you already own. Start with one decision question and ask it to show the context it found before answering.

## What you'll accomplish

<Check>
  You'll start an AI Now task that identifies the saved context relevant to one real question.

## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Now">
    In Mem, open the **AI Now** tab in the sidebar. You can also press `Cmd/Ctrl` + `5`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question tied to your knowledge">
    Use a question with a specific decision and time frame. For example:

    ```text
    We are considering an annual planning review for Atlas. What decisions, plans, or customer insights in Nowledge Mem are relevant? List the evidence first. Do not recommend a direction yet.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the context before moving on">
    Confirm that the answer identifies material you recognize. If one important memory is missing, mention it with `@` or add it before continuing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  AI Now returns relevant saved knowledge and makes clear what it used as context for the task.
</Check>

## Why this matters

Starting with grounded context keeps the task connected to your actual history. It gives you a better question to investigate than a broad request for advice.

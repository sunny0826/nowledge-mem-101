## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose one primary source">
    Use a document that is directly relevant to the decision, such as customer interview notes, a CSV of support requests, or a project brief. Avoid uploading material you are not allowed to share with your configured AI provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the file or use the Library">
    Drop the file into the AI Now conversation. If it is already in the Library, refer to it by name instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell AI Now how to use it">
    Continue the Atlas task with a specific instruction:

    ```text
    Use the attached customer interview notes with the Atlas context already found. Identify statements that support, challenge, or leave open the case for an annual planning review. Cite the interview or section when possible.
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  AI Now uses the source and your saved context together, rather than answering only from a generic model response.
</Check>

<Note>
Attached files are copied into the current task workspace. When attachment is unavailable, add the file to the Library and refer to it there.
</Note>

## Why this matters

A primary source gives the task evidence. It lets you distinguish what the material says from what you or the AI infer from it.

## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Separate the durable result from the draft">
    Keep only facts a future session needs: the decision, the approved output or its location, and the next action. Do not preserve unfinished alternatives as if they were settled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the final update in Timeline">
    Adapt this example:

    ```text
    Atlas landing page update

    Final decision: the page opens with the cost of status chasing and then shows the weekly planning flow.
    Completed: six-section outline, hero copy, and section four copy are ready for review.
    Next step: have the product owner review the proof point in section four before publishing.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the restart point">
    In a new session, ask a connected AI:

    ```text
    What is the current status and next step for the Atlas landing page?
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  The AI describes the completed work and the remaining next step from the final update, without treating old drafts as the final decision.
</Check>

## Why this matters

Closing the loop keeps useful context current. Each pass leaves a clearer starting point for the next one, whether you return tomorrow, switch tools, or hand the work to someone else.

## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a fresh session in your second connected AI">
    Choose a different AI tool connected to the same Mem. Do not copy the original work brief or the first tool's conversation into this session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it to recover the task state">
    Use a focused continuation prompt:

    ```text
    Continue the Atlas landing page work. Read the work brief and latest handoff checkpoint in Nowledge Mem. State the goal, constraints, and next step you found. Then write the hero and section four copy.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare its context summary with the checkpoint">
    Verify that the tool found the settled choice and the unfinished proof point before it writes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the new output">
    Check that the copy follows the original audience and language constraints. Revise it in the tool if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  The second AI can explain the task state from Mem and produce the next artifact without you rebuilding the full context.
</Check>

## Why this matters

This is the workflow payoff: tools can change while the task remains understandable. The shared element is your preserved knowledge, not an invisible shared chat window.

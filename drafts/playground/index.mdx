This page hosts the embeddable Playground component. Save a memory, then ask a question to recall it — the same loop as Lesson 1 and Lesson 2, without opening the app. Any lesson can embed the same component with `<Playground />`.

<Note>
Everything here runs locally in your browser with sample data. Nothing is sent anywhere, and refreshing the page resets the demo.

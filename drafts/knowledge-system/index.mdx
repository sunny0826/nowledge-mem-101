## What you'll build

By the end of this course, one topic you care about will have a small, working knowledge system:

```text
Topic → Durable units → Source → Explicit link → Evolution → Retrieval test
```

The examples use the Atlas team's annual planning process. Replace it with a long-running project, customer, research area, or operating practice from your own work.

## Before you start

- Complete the [Essentials course](/essentials).
- Keep at least five memories about one real topic in the same Space. Default is fine.
- Prepare one document that contains useful source material for that topic.
- If Background Intelligence is available in your build, configure a Remote LLM and enable it if you want Wiki topics and knowledge-evolution links to update during the course.

<Note>
Do not create a Space only to make the system look organized. Stay in Default until two real contexts keep getting in each other's way.
</Note>

## What this course does not ask you to do

You do not need a perfect label taxonomy, a folder for every topic, or a manual wiki. Mem handles topic grouping and graph maintenance in the background. Your job is to choose worthwhile sources, keep durable units clear, and make deliberate relationships explicit.

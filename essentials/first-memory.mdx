Mem's value isn't just taking notes. Information you leave today can still be found by you and your AI in the future.

## What you'll accomplish

<Check>
  You'll leave your first memory in Mem that's truly yours.
</Check>

## Watch how it's done

<video controls src="https://nmem-101.guoxudong.io/videos/first-memory.mp4" />

## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Nowledge Mem">
    Go to Timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Think of something real worth keeping today">
    It can be a decision, an insight, a project's context, or information you'll need later.

    Use your own real content if you can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it down and save it">
    Type it in the Timeline input, then press Enter.

    ```text
    We decided the new marketing site uses Next.js because we need SSR and better SEO.
    ```

    <Tip>
      You can copy the example, but your own real information works better.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  The content you just saved appears in Timeline.

  If you can see it, you've completed the first step.
</Check>

<Note>
  Can't see it? Make sure the content was submitted and isn't still sitting in the input box.
</Note>

## Why this matters

What you just left isn't a temporary input. This information can later be searched, linked to other knowledge, and reused by the AI tools connected to Mem.

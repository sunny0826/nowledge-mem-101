So far, you have saved short memories. But knowledge often lives in longer material: reports, specs, PDFs, and web pages. Add the source itself instead of summarizing it by hand, then let Mem search it.

## Why this matters

Mem searches documents and memories together. The source stays intact, so you and your AI can later reference and reuse its details without having to remember or explain them again.

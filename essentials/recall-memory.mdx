## Your turn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remember what you saved in the last lesson">
    Don't look through your history first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question only that memory can answer">
    Type the question in Timeline.

    If you saved:

    ```text
    We decided the new marketing site uses Next.js because we need SSR and better SEO.
    ```

    Now ask:

    ```text
    What technology did we decide on for the new site?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the question">
    Watch what Mem returns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should see

<Check>
  Mem found the relevant memory and used it to answer your question.
</Check>

Capture → Recall

This is Mem's most basic loop: leave knowledge first, then bring it back when you need it.

## Why this matters

A truly valuable knowledge system doesn't ask you to remember where information lives. It brings the right information back when you need it.

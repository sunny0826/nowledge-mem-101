## What you should see

<Check>
  The brief separates a reason to test the idea from proof that it works. Each finding has a source, and the next uncertainty has a clear way to investigate it.
</Check>

## Why this matters

An evidence brief is more useful than a polished answer with hidden assumptions. It gives you a clear boundary between the knowledge you own and the information you still need.

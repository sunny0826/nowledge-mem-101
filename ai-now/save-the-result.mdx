Save the checked outcome of your Nowledge Mem AI Now task. Include the evidence you used and any gap that research or an internal follow-up still needs to resolve.

## What you'll accomplish

<Check>
  You'll save a checked result in Mem, then find it and reread its source from a fresh conversation.
</Check>

## Learn with the simulation

On desktop, the simulation opens automatically when you enter this lesson. Follow the popup hints to send a request, read its reply, and check the result. If you close it, click **Start the simulation** under [Your turn](#your-turn) to reopen it. Each lesson prepares its fictional Atlas materials; no model setup is needed.

<Note>
The simulation runs locally with preset replies. It does not call AI or search the web, and refreshing resets the practice. On mobile, read the steps first and use a desktop browser for the interactive exercise.
</Note>

## Before practising in the real app

Have the evidence brief and the finding or open question from lesson 4 ready. Public research is optional; record only the work you actually completed. Keep the lesson 2 source available in Library. A saved conversation history and a durable memory are different: this lesson saves a takeaway that can stand on its own.

## What you should see

<Check>
  A fresh conversation finds the record with its status, evidence, limits, and next action, and rereads the original source. It does not turn your recommendation into an approved decision.
</Check>

<Note>
If retrieval fails, first confirm that the memory exists in the current Space. Search by its title; if needed, select it with `@` and ask again. Record whether it was found unaided or read after an explicit mention. Pasting the result back into the conversation does not verify retrieval, and saving another copy does not fix it.
</Note>

<Tip>
Correct wording mistakes in the existing memory. When a real decision changes later, save a dated update that names the earlier record and explains the change, so you can still trace the history.
</Tip>

## Why this matters

The result becomes useful beyond this AI Now session. Future tasks and AI tools connected to the same Mem can start from the result record and read the Library source when they need more detail.
